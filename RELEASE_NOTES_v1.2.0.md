# ArthroHarm v1.2.0

This is the first public, versioned release of the frozen ArthroHarm v1.2 research prototype evaluated in the associated methodology study.

## What is included

- exact evaluated extractor and rule files;
- exact automatic matcher and scoring core;
- label-free HTML and PDF normalization adapters;
- capsule-freeze, prediction-seal, scoring-wrapper and reproducibility-verification utilities;
- 37 passing synthetic unit/regression tests;
- synthetic runnable example;
- SHA-256 provenance and rights-boundary documentation.

## Important limitation

The locked Stage II primary event-level recall was 28.01%. This release must not be used autonomously or for clinical decision making. It is provided for methods research and constrained human-in-the-loop evaluation.

Publisher full texts and substantial verbatim excerpts are excluded. Exact corpus reruns require lawful access to the same report versions.
